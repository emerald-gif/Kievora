✦ FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know about Kievora pricing.

Is Free really free forever? ⌄
Yes. The Free plan has no time limit and no credit card required. You can build resumes and use KIE to prep for your job search without ever paying.
Can I cancel anytime? ⌄
Absolutely. Cancel anytime from your account settings — no cancellation fees, no questions asked. You'll keep Pro/Premier access until the end of your billing period.
What's the difference between KIE Spark, Core, and Nova? ⌄
Spark is our fast, lightweight model for everyday questions. Core handles deeper analysis like resume reviews and interview prep. Nova is our most powerful model — available exclusively on Premier — for complex career strategy and high-stakes applications.
Do you offer refunds? ⌄
If you're not satisfied within the first 7 days of a paid plan, reach out to support and we'll make it right. We stand behind the product.
Is there a student discount? ⌄
We're working on it. In the meantime, the Free plan covers most of what students need. Reach out to support with your student email and we'll see what we can do.
