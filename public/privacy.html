Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 1, 2026

At Kievora, your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data when you use our platform at kievora.app.

The short version: We collect only what we need to run the service, we never sell your data, and you can delete your account and data at any time.

1 Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and information generated through your use of the platform:

TypeWhat we collectWhy
Account infoName, email address, profile photo (if using Google Sign-In)To create and manage your account
Resume dataWork history, education, skills, personal summary, contact details you enterTo build and store your resumes
Usage dataPages visited, features used, session duration, device type, browserTo improve the platform and fix issues
AI interactionsPrompts and messages sent to KIE AITo generate responses and improve AI quality
Uploaded filesResumes you upload for analysisTo perform resume analysis and scoring

2 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Kievora platform
  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account
  • Generate AI-powered resume suggestions and career coaching via KIE
  • Send transactional emails such as account confirmations and welcome messages
  • Respond to your support requests and communications
  • Analyse usage patterns to improve features and fix bugs
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3 Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore), a cloud database service with enterprise-grade security. Resume data is stored per user account and is only accessible to you when authenticated.

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data including encrypted connections (HTTPS), Firebase Authentication for secure sign-in, and access controls that restrict data to the authenticated user only.

While we take security seriously, no system is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.

4 Third-Party Services

Kievora uses trusted third-party services to operate. Each handles data according to their own privacy policies

We do not share identifiable personal data with these services beyond what is necessary to provide the service.

5 Cookies & Tracking

Kievora uses minimal cookies and local storage to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

Firebase Authentication uses browser storage to maintain your session. You can clear this by logging out or clearing your browser data.

6 Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — Update or correct inaccurate personal information
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Portability — Request your resume data in a portable format
  • Objection — Object to certain uses of your data

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@kievora.app. We will respond within 30 days.

7 Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data and resumes within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

Anonymous, aggregated usage data (with no personally identifiable information) may be retained indefinitely for platform improvement purposes.

8 Children's Privacy

Kievora is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you by email or with a notice on the platform. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision.

Continued use of Kievora after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
