✦ Questions

Before you connect

Is my Gmail data safe?
⌄
KIE only reads emails it classifies as job-search related — confirmations, recruiter replies, interview invites. Nothing is shared, sold, or used to train external models.
What does KIE actually read?
⌄
Only emails KIE detects as job-search related. Personal, financial, and unrelated emails are ignored entirely and never processed.
Can I disconnect anytime?
⌄
Yes. One click in Settings revokes access immediately, and KIE clears its synced data within 24 hours.
Does KIE send emails for me?
⌄
Not yet. Today KIE drafts everything — follow-ups, replies — for you to review and send yourself. Auto-send is on the roadmap.
Is this free?
⌄
Yes. Gmail integration is part of Kievora's free plan, like everything else core to your job search.
